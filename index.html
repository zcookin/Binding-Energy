Global Minimum
Iron-56 · 8.79 MeV/nucleon
True nuclear ground state. All stellar nucleosynthesis terminates here. Stars cannot release energy by fusing beyond iron.
Local Minimum — U-235 chain endpoint
Lead-207 · 7.87 MeV/nucleon
More stable than uranium but not the true ground state. The potential barrier to iron is insurmountably high on any observable timescale.
Starting point — high potential energy
U-235 · 7.59 MeV/nucleon
Rolls down ~15 α/β/γ resets toward Pb-207. Fission jumps ~200 MeV toward the iron valley by splitting into mid-mass fragments.
